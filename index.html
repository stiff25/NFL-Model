How are strength categories decided?

STRONG: at least 72% model probability for the chosen winner and unanimous votes when available. QUALIFIED: at least 60% with model agreement. UPSET: the model picks the market's moneyline underdog. LEAN: all other outright selections or disagreement. An upset can be high confidence, but the UPSET label takes priority so it stands out. ATS PASS does not mean skipping your Pick’em contest.
